publication: "The Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization, Vol. 40, Issue 3, Nov. 2024"
abstract: "Exploiting umpiring reforms in international cricket, where two umpires make independent decisions in each other's presence, this paper shows that home-team umpires are less biased when working with a neutral colleague — one who is a national of neither team. The temporary debiasing is driven by social pressure to appear impartial in front of a neutral peer."
