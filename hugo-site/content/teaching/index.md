## Advising

Fernando has supervised six undergraduate honors theses at Notre Dame, including two BonAnno Thesis
Prize winners (Daniel Martin, ND &rsquo;16; James Mancini, ND &rsquo;23) and one honorable mention
(Alison Lodermeier, ND &rsquo;17, now at the University of Chicago). His Ph.D. advisees include Paul
Shaloka (ND &rsquo;23, now at the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission) and Linh Nguyen (ND
&rsquo;20, now at Bates College). Former research assistants and pre-doctoral fellows have gone on to
economics Ph.D. programs at the University of Michigan and Brown University, among other placements.
